# Bettter.md — Pessimistic Review of Pygin (v55 era)

> **Date:** 2026-07-26  
> **Engine:** ~2885 Elo, ~4.3M nps, 54 A/B-confirmed versions, built by 35k+ lines of C + Python.  
> **Scope:** csearch.c, cengine.py, engine.py, cuci.py, match.py, eval_c.c, the NNUE scaffold, the testing harness.  
> **Attitude:** Pessimistic. Every estimate assumes the worst plausible case. Times are single-developer, first-attempt. "Where" points at file:line or feature.

| 1 | **History induction from the Syzygy TB probe** — the root TB probe (difficulty-gated, only for positions < ~500cp) returns a proven-optimal move. | 6 | **`has_legal_quiet` bulk-skip for pawnless endgames** — `has_legal_quiet` generates all quiet non-pawn moves AND all single pawn pushes to check if any legal quiet exists. ## Summary of the State of Pygin

**The engine is solid.** 54 A/B-confirmed versions, six SPRT accepts, ~2885 Elo, the C core is clean, the eval is well-tuned, and the A/B methodology is best-in-class for a solo project. The pessimism above is about **edges, not foundations**.

### What's Actually Hurting Right Now

1. **The NNUE pipeline hasn't produced a real net** (Phase 6-8 not started). The ~300 Elo from a properly trained NNUE is the biggest remaining gain, and it's completely untouched. Every week of dataset collection is a week closer.

2. **Search-side improvements are exhausted at this TC.** The 0.5m+ ledger of dormant mechanisms (FI-04, FI-06, FI-12, FI-18, FI-23, FI-48, FI-49, FI-55, FI-59, FI-63, FI-64, P-04, P-33, P-43, Q-01) makes this clear: at 50+0.20 / ~1.75M nodes per move, the search has converged. The remaining gains are in **eval retuning, NPS, and NNUE**.

3. **The A/B velocity has collapsed.** v51→v52→v53→v54→v55 produced +82 Elo in <2 weeks (FI-56 11.12 + FI-24 6.63 + v53 37.52 + v54 31.20 + v55 9.66). But v53 and v54 were the FIRST eval retunes ever — a backlog item. v55 was a pure speed pair. The next 5 versions (v56-v60) are unlikely to average more than +5 per version unless the eval is retuned again or the NNUE pipeline delivers.
